\section{Algebra skills}

Simplify the following expresisons,
\begin{align*}
    1) &\quad x-\left[ 3a + 2(-x+1)\right], \\
    2) &\quad -\left[3x-2y+(x+2y)-2(x+y)-3(x+1)\right], \\
    3) &\quad a-(x+y) -3(x-y) +2\left[-(x-2y)-2(-x-y)\right].
\end{align*}

Find all real zeros of the following polynomials,
\begin{align*}
    1) &\quad P(x) = 3x^3 + 5x^2 -2x - 4 \\
    2) &\quad P(x) = 3x^4 -5x^3 -16x^2 +7x + 15 \\
    3) &\quad P(x) = x^4 -6x^3 + 4x^2 +15 x + 4 \\
    4) &\quad P(x) = 3x^3 + 4x^2 -x -2 
\end{align*}

\section{Function analysis}

Mention the domain and range of the following functions,
\begin{align*}
    1) \quad f(x) = \sqrt{x^2 -1}, \qquad 
    2) \quad g(x) = \frac{2}{x(3x+5)}, \qquad
    3) \quad h(x) = -\frac{3}{2}x^2.
\end{align*}

\section{Modelling with math}

\paragraph{a)} A school fund-raising group sells chocolate bars to help ﬁnance a swimming pool for their physical education program. 
The group ﬁnds that when they set their price at $x$ dollars per bar (where $0<x\leq5$), their total sales revenue (in dollars) is given by the function $R(x) = -500x^2 + 3000x$.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Evaluate $R(2)$ and $R(4)$. What do these values represent?
    \item How many bars they need to sell to get even?
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{b)}  A right triangle has one leg twice as long as the other. Find a function that
­models its perimeter P in terms of the length x of the shorter leg.

\paragraph{c)} A Norman window has the shape of a rectangle surmounted by a semicircle. 
A Norman window with perimeter 30 ft is to be constructed.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Find a function that models the area of the window.
    \item Find the dimensions of the window that admits the greatest amount of light.
\end{itemize}
