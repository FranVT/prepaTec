\begin{frame}{Critical Points: Class Activity}

    Compute the derivative of the functions
    \begin{align*}
        f(x) &= xe^{x} \\
        g(x) &= -4.9 x^2 + 98x + 10 
    \end{align*}
    Then find the critical points of the functions.
    Finally, evaluate the functions at the critical points in order to find the maximum or the minimum of the functions.
