\paragraph{Actually the motivation}
At some point in the history of humankind there was two open questions: How do you find the slope of acurve at a single, exact point? and How do you measure the area of a shape with a curved boundary?
The first question was askd by the greeks.
They where studying lines that only touches once a circle or most commonly kwnow as the tangent line to a circle.
However, for other kind of graphs it was a mystery, like for a straight line, a parabola or an ellipse.
After some centuries, the answer to this question led us to the development of the derivative.
On the other hand, the second question led to the creation of the integral.
In a more specific tale, Archimedes was a master of computing areas under parabolas, however its method wasa headache.
Some years later, astronomers like Kepler need to calculated areas of planetary orbits and this was solved by the definition of an integral.

Integral allow us to give a quantitative answer to questions like "How much in total?", meanwhile, derivatives allow us to give a quantitative answer to questions that ask "How much change in total?".

And we can comine both, "How much change in total the total amount of something?"
